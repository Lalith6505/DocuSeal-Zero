Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Absolute Confidentiality & Cleanroom Integrity

DocuSeal Zero is designed specifically for high-security environments, including border checkpoints, banking vaults, forensic document examination laboratories, and notary offices. We recognize that examined specimens—such as biometric passports, banknotes, confidential legal deeds, and identity cards—contain sensitive personal and proprietary information. DocuSeal Zero does not transmit, store, or forward any camera frames, scanned imagery, or inspection metadata to remote servers or cloud services.

2. Camera & Hardware Sensor Permissions

The application requires camera access exclusively for real-time optical magnification and 2D spatial FFT texture analysis. All optical computations are executed entirely in volatile memory on the local device NPU/GPU and are discarded immediately after rendering. Flashlight control is utilized strictly for specimen illumination.

3. Zero Ads, Zero Trackers, Zero Third-Party SDKs

DocuSeal Zero contains zero commercial advertising frameworks, zero behavioural analytics trackers, and zero third-party diagnostic SDKs. The application operates in complete network isolation and functions without any active internet connection.

4. Local Cryptographic Audit Storage

All verification certificates, SHA-256 hashes, and operator notes saved in the Forensic Audit Vault are stored in the application's local sandboxed directory on your Android device. You maintain exclusive ownership and control over all audit records, with full authority to export or delete them at any time.

5. Policy Inquiries

For any inquiries regarding this policy or technical architecture, you may inspect the open source documentation repository on GitHub or contact the developer directly via the Google Play Store developer portal.
